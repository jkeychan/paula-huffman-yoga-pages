About Paula

Creating space for every student, built on 40+ years of teaching experience.

Paula Huffman began her journey into mind-body practice through yoga in her twenties, sparking a lifelong commitment to contemplative practice. She earned her BS in Special Education from Appalachian State University, then completed a nursing degree and holds RN licensure in North Carolina, alongside a 200-hour Yoga Teacher certification and multiple specialty trainings. For 25 years she ran a perinatal support practice (prenatal and postnatal yoga, childbirth education, and birth doula care), and during that time co-founded Hillsborough Yoga and Healing Arts, which she owned and operated for seven years. She went on to complete MBSR Teacher Training through the Center for Mindfulness at UMass Medical School’s Oasis Program, then taught Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction for sixteen years with UNC Chapel Hill’s Program on Integrative Medicine, where she also holds an Adjunct Faculty appointment in the Department of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation. Today she brings that same depth to her own independent practice, Carolina Mindfulness.

She keeps her classes quiet and unhurried, and she adjusts the practice to fit each student instead of asking students to fit the practice.

Offerings

Services

MBSR: 8-Week Program

This full 8-week MBSR program, developed by Jon Kabat-Zinn, sets the gold standard for mindfulness training. Includes a one-hour complimentary orientation session, eight 2.5-hour sessions, and an in-person day retreat. You'll receive home practice guides and references for online supporting materials, including practice recordings. Delivered by Zoom.

MBSR: Adapted Program for Busy Times

Inspired by the evidence-based principles and practices of MBSR, offering a flexible format designed to meet the needs of today's participants. The course integrates mindfulness meditation, gentle mindful movement, body awareness, and practical strategies for responding to stress with greater clarity, resilience, and self-compassion. Six weeks, 2-hour weekly sessions, with home practice guides and references for online supporting materials, including practice recordings.

Specialty Workshops & Mini Courses

1.5–2 hour workshops and mini-courses in both yoga and mindfulness, offered in person and via live Zoom on varying topics including Yoga & Mindfulness for Sleep, Cultivating Mindfulness in Everyday Life, a complimentary introduction to mindfulness, and more.

Brain Health & Wellness Course

An experiential four-week, 1.5-hour-session course led by Paula as a Certified Brain Health Specialist. You'll learn more about:

  • Cognitive decline: is it aging or something else?
  • Keeping our brains and body healthy
  • Brain health is heart health

Includes complimentary Zoom yoga and meditation sessions, with monthly follow-up workshops.

Stretch Strengthen Restore Live Zoom Yoga

Practice yoga in the comfort of your own home! Weekly one-hour Alignment-Based Hatha Yoga, suitable for all ages and levels of practice, with adaptations offered to meet each student where they are. Monthly restorative yoga classes. Your first Hatha Yoga class is complimentary!

Seasonal Wellness Retreat

An all-day in-person retreat held at New Hope Camp & Conference Center in Chapel Hill. Discover the nurturing effects of being in nature while enjoying the beauty of each season. Activities include Community Resilience Method practices, a body scan, yoga, walking meditation, seated meditation, and loving-kindness meditation, wrapped around time for socialization and solitude.

Yoga & Meditation Beach Retreat

A 2–3 night residential retreat at Trinity Center in Salter Path, NC, co-led with Ann Burrus, E‑RYT: yoga for all levels, meditation, and rest by the sea. A cozy respite for the winter doldrums, with private or semi-private rooms, private baths, and all meals included (special diets available).

Specialty & Private Programs

Customized sessions for home-school groups, book clubs, corporate teams, educators, students, and healthcare practitioners, virtual or on-location.

What Is Mindfulness?

Paying attention, on purpose

Mindfulness is the practice of paying attention to the present moment, on purpose and without judgment. It's a trainable skill, not a personality trait. It develops through consistent practice, and it's useful for stress, pain, sleep, and simply meeting daily life with more steadiness.

Benefits of Yoga

Strength, flexibility, and calm

Beyond flexibility and strength, a regular yoga practice supports better sleep, lower stress and blood pressure, improved balance, and a steadier nervous system. Paula's gentle, alignment-based approach makes these benefits accessible at any age or fitness level.
